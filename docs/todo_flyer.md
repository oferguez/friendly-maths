# Flyer To-Do

## Keep

-   Keep the main headline **"להבין במקום לשנן"** exactly as it is.
-   Keep the green colour palette and overall visual style.
-   Keep the QR code and contact details prominent.
-   Refine the existing design rather than redesigning it from scratch.

## Content changes

-   Replace **"שיעורים גמישים"** with something more concrete,
    preferably **"אונליין או פנים אל פנים"**.
-   Review the small explanatory text in the three boxes: increase its
    size by around 15--20%, or shorten it.
-   Give the qualifications/experience section more visual importance.
-   Consider removing **"תואר שני בפילוסופיה"**. The maths degree and
    tutoring experience are more relevant.
-   Suggested credentials:
    -   **"תואר ראשון במתמטיקה ומדעי המחשב"**
    -   **"ניסיון בהוראת GCSE, שיעורים פרטיים והתנדבות ב-Action
        Tutoring"**
-   If aimed at Israeli families in London, replace **"הכנה לבגרות"**
    with British-system terminology, e.g. **"GCSE ו-A-level, סגירת פערים
    ועזרה שוטפת"**.

## Call to action

-   Consider replacing **"נשמע מתאים?"** with **"רוצים לדבר?"** or
    **"רוצים לשמוע עוד?"**
-   Suggested follow-up: **"התקשרו או שלחו הודעה, ונתאם שיעור ניסיון
    חינם."**

## Layout and mobile readability

-   Export the final WhatsApp flyer at **1080 × 1350 px (4:5
    portrait)**.
-   Do not simply stretch the current design vertically.
-   Use the extra vertical space to separate:
    1.  Headline and introduction
    2.  Main benefits/services
    3.  Qualifications and experience
    4.  Contact / call to action
-   Increase the smallest text sizes; they are currently borderline for
    comfortable phone reading.
-   Check the final export at actual WhatsApp/mobile size before
    distributing it.
